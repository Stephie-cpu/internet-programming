A TRIBUTE TO PATRICE LUMUMBA

Patrice Emery Lumumba was a congolese Politician ,indipendence leader,African nationalist,and Pan-africanist.
He was born on JULY 2, 1925.He served as the first democratically elected prime minister of the republic of Congo.
.He is remembered as one of the mosticonic figures in Africas strugggle against colonialism from the harsh belgium rule
In the period following World War II, young leaders across Africa increasingly worked for national goals and independence from the colonial powers.
In 1952 he was hired to work as a personal assistant for French sociologist Pierre Clément, who was performing a study of Stanleyville.
That year he also co-founded and subsequently became president of a Stanleyville chapter of the Association des Anciens élèves des pères de Scheut (ADAPÉS), an alumni association for former students at Scheut schools, even though he had never attended one.
In 1955, Lumumba became regional head of the Cercles of Stanleyville and joined the Liberal Party of Belgium.
He edited and distributed party literature. Between 1956 and 1957 he wrote his autobiography (which was published posthumously in 1962).
After a study tour in Belgium in 1956, he was arrested on charges of embezzlement of $2500 from the post office.
He was convicted and sentenced one year later to 12 months' imprisonment and a fine.

LEGACY

Patrice Lumumba was only 35 years old when he died.Despite his short life and brief time in power ,he became a global symbol of:

  1. African Nationalism
  2. Anti colonialism resistance
  3. Pan-African unity
  4. The fight for geniune independence and economic sovreignty
